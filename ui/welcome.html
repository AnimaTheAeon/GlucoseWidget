Welcome to Glucose Widget 👋

This little box sits on your desktop and shows your Dexcom glucose number. It dings when you go too high or too low, so you notice even while you're working.

This guide takes about 5 minutes. You'll need:

  1. Your phone with the Dexcom app on it (the one that talks to the sensor).
  2. Your Dexcom username and password, the same ones you use in that app.
Please read: this widget is a helper, not a medical device. Keep the alarms on your phone or receiver turned on, and always make treatment decisions from your Dexcom app, not from this widget.

Which Dexcom do you use?

Pick the app on your phone that shows your glucose.

Sorry, Stelo can't be used. This widget gets your numbers through Dexcom's Share feature, and the Stelo app doesn't have Share. You can still finish the guide if you also use Nightscout.

Turn on Share on your phone

The widget reads your numbers from Dexcom's Share feature (the same one the Dexcom Follow app uses). Share only sends numbers when at least one follower has accepted an invitation.

  1. Open the Dexcom G7 app.
  2. Tap Connections at the bottom, then tap Share.
  3. If it's your first time, read the screens and tap Next.
  4. Tap Invite Follower, type a name and email, and send the invitation.
  5. The follower opens the email and taps Accept invitation in the Dexcom Follow app.
  6. Back on the Share screen, the Status should say Working.
  1. Open the Dexcom G6 app.
  2. Tap Share (on the main screen or in the menu).
  3. Turn Share on and tap Invite Followers.
  4. Type the follower's name and email and send the invitation.
  5. The follower opens the email and taps Accept invitation in the Dexcom Follow app.
No one to invite? Invite yourself using a second email address, then accept it in the free Dexcom Follow app. That's enough for the widget to work.
Keep your phone sharing: it needs internet, Bluetooth on, and the Dexcom app running in the background. On iPhone, leave Background App Refresh on for the Dexcom app and avoid Low Power Mode, or numbers can arrive late.

Log in to Dexcom

Use the same login as your Dexcom app, the account of the person wearing the sensor. Not a follower's login.

Saved on this PC only, locked by Windows. It is never sent anywhere except Dexcom.

Type your Nightscout website address.

When should it ding?

The widget dings and flashes when your number goes past these. You can change them any time in Settings.

Not sure? Ask your doctor what your low and high numbers should be.

It also warns you if no new number arrives for 20 minutes.

Pick a look

Choose how the widget looks. You can switch any time in Settings.

All set! 🎉

Click Finish and your widget appears in the bottom-right corner of your screen.

  1. Drag it anywhere you like. It remembers the spot.
  2. 🔔 Bell = snooze the dings for 30 minutes.
  3. ⚙ Gear = Settings (change your login, alerts or look).
  4. ⬇ Arrow = hide it. It pops back up by itself if you go high or low.
  5. Right-click it for more, like "Start with Windows".
You can open this guide again any time: right-click the widget and pick Setup guide.
