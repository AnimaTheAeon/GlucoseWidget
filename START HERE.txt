GLUCOSE WIDGET - START HERE
===========================

What this is
------------
A little box that sits on your desktop and shows your Dexcom number.
It dings when you go ABOVE 250 or BELOW 70, and it warns you if the
numbers stop coming in.

It is a helper, not a medical device. Keep the alarms on your phone or
receiver turned on. Never dose insulin from this widget alone.


COMING FROM THE OLD (BIG) VERSION?
----------------------------------
1. Right-click the old widget and pick Quit.
2. Delete the old "Glucose Widget-win32-x64" folder.
3. Set up this one below. Your theme, alert numbers, username and
   widget spot are copied over by themselves. You only need to type
   your Dexcom password again (the old version locked it in a way
   only it could open).


ONE-TIME SETUP (about 5 minutes)
--------------------------------

STEP 1 - On your iPhone: turn on Share
   1. Open the Dexcom app (G6 or G7).
   2. Tap "Share" (G6: the triangle-of-dots icon. G7: Connections > Share).
   3. Turn Share on and invite at least one follower.
      It can be a family member or your own second email address.
      The widget only gets numbers if Share is on with a follower.
   Note: Dexcom Stelo has no Share, so it cannot work with this widget.

STEP 2 - On your PC: put the folder somewhere it can live forever
   1. Move the whole "GlucoseWidget" folder to a permanent
      spot, for example  C:\GlucoseWidget
      (Do this BEFORE the first run. The widget starts itself with
      Windows from wherever it was first run.)

STEP 3 - Start it
   1. Open the folder and double-click  "GlucoseWidget.exe"
   2. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
      click "More info", then "Run anyway". (It shows this for any
      app that was not bought from a big company.)

STEP 4 - Follow the setup guide
   1. The first time it starts, a "Welcome" window opens by itself.
   2. It walks you through everything: turning on Share on your
      phone, typing your Dexcom login, testing it, picking when it
      should ding, and choosing a look. Click "Next" on each page.
   3. Click "Finish" at the end.
   (Want to see it again later? Right-click the widget > Setup guide.)

That's it. The number shows up in the box within a few seconds.


USING IT
--------
- Drag the box anywhere. It remembers where you put it.
- Bell button    = snooze the dings for 30 minutes (click again to undo).
- Gear button    = settings.
- Dash button    = hide it. It tucks into the little icon near the clock
                   (no taskbar icon). Click that icon to bring it back.
                   It keeps watching and pops back up and dings if you
                   go high or low. To use the normal taskbar instead,
                   untick "Minimise to tray" in Settings.
- Right-click the box (or the small icon near the clock) for the menu:
  Always on top, Start with Windows, Quit.
- The small icon near the clock also shows your number.

Adding or removing looks
------------------------
Every look except Classic is one small file in the "themes" folder,
inside the app folder. Want fewer looks? Delete that look's file.
Want it back? Put the file back. Then open Settings and the list
updates. (Classic is built in and can't be removed, so the widget
always has something to show.)

  Every file is named after its look, for example
  "Green Orb.js" is the Green Orb look. What you see in
  Settings is the file's name.

Only put theme files here that came from Claude or that you trust,
because they run inside the widget.

The looks (pick in Settings > Look > Theme):
- Classic        = dark box with a 3-hour graph.
- Reactor Timer  = blue box with digital-clock digits. The small timer
                   counts down to your next reading (one comes every
                   5 minutes). If it says LATE, the reading did not come
                   and the timer counts up instead. In this look the
                   in-range number is white instead of green.
- Farm Sign      = wooden sign on a starry sky, pixel-art style.
                   The sky turns sunset for high and red for low.
- Night City HUD = dark red screen with a menu list. The one you are in
                   gets the cyan box. Blue in range, yellow high, red low.
- Green Orb      = glowing orb on a dark green dashboard. The glowing
                   tube along the bottom is your last 3 hours, with
                   light flowing along it. The two dotted lines are
                   your high and low limits.
- Liquid Glass   = dark, shiny glass slab like the newest iPhone
                   widgets. The glow inside turns green, orange, red
                   or gray with your number.

Colors:  green = in range   orange = high   red = low
         gray  = the number is old (no new data)

What the dings mean:
- Fast triple beeps  = LOW  (repeats every 5 min until you are back up)
- Ding-dong          = HIGH (repeats every 15 min)
- Two soft beeps     = no new data for 20 minutes, or a login problem


IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
---------------------
"No new data" / gray number
   Your phone is what sends numbers to Dexcom. Check that the phone has
   internet, Bluetooth is on, and the Dexcom app is open in the
   background. The widget catches up by itself.

"No connection, retrying"
   Your PC cannot reach Dexcom right now. It keeps retrying by itself.

"Login problem: open Settings"
   The username or password was not accepted. Fix it in Settings.
   The widget stops trying on purpose, because too many wrong tries
   can lock your Dexcom account for a while.

No sound
   Check the PC volume and speakers. Windows "Do not disturb" hides the
   pop-up banner, but the widget's own ding still plays.

Still stuck
   Right-click the box > "Open log file" and send that file to Claude.
   (Your password is never written in it.)


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Dexcom makes one new number every 5 minutes. The widget checks right
  after each one is due, so it is usually under a minute behind your phone.
- Your password is saved only on this PC, scrambled by Windows.
- If the PC is asleep or off, the widget cannot ding.
- If you ever move the folder, right-click > untick and re-tick
  "Start with Windows".
